## 🙋‍♂️ About Me

Hi, I'm **Fanny Bagus Ramadhan**, a fullstack developer and content creator from East Java, Indonesia, with 4+ years of experience building production web applications.

- 🏦 Fullstack Developer (R&D) at [Digitalku](https://digitalku.com), a web-hosting provider with 10,000+ clients, where I own system design and feature planning for its core platforms
- 🌱 Lately exploring Claude and other AI tools, Laravel, React, TypeScript, Fastify, and AdonisJS
- 🎓 Studying Informatics Engineering part-time at Universitas Dian Nuswantoro (UDINUS)
- 🎥 Making anime and tech content on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@WibuDenial) when inspiration strikes
- 🖥️ Portfolio, blog, and notes at [fanny.dev](https://www.fanny.dev/), with my [resume](https://www.fanny.dev/resume/) too
- 📫 Say hello at [me@fanny.dev](mailto:me@fanny.dev)
